## 💡 Key Business Insights & Strategic Impact

### 1. Revenue & Target Gap Analysis
* **Insight:** The business achieved **95.4%** of its annual revenue target (**527K** actual vs. **552K** target), leaving a manageable **-25K variance**, while maintaining a strong **36.9% Gross Margin**.
* **Business Value:** Pinpointed specific seasonal dips (such as **June**) where net sales dropped sharply, allowing leadership to plan mid-year promotional buffers to close the 25K gap.

### 2. Marketing Spend & Campaign Efficiency
* **Insight:** Marketing utilized **97.5%** of its allocated budget (**79K** out of **81K**), generating **7M impressions** and a healthy **3.19% overall CTR**. Top-performing campaigns like **Back to School** and **Black Friday** drove the highest click-through rates, while **Spring Launch** led in target attainment.
* **Business Value:** Enables the marketing team to reallocate budget away from lower-converting campaigns (e.g., *Summer Sale* at 2.88% CTR) toward high-ROI seasonal spikes.

### 3. Customer Retention & Pareto Concentration
* **Insight:** Out of **60 total customers**, only **29 (48.3%) are currently active**, yet they generate an average of **8.98K** in net sales per customer. Key accounts like **Cascade Comm** and **Meridian Partners** dominate gross sales.
* **Business Value:** Highlighted a major reactivation opportunity—winning back even 20% of the 31 inactive accounts would easily cover the **25K revenue variance**.

### 4. Regional & Sales Team Performance
* **Insight:** **Europe** and **Asia Pacific** lead in both gross sales and target attainment, while **Latin America** lags behind. Among account managers, **Tom Reyes** drives the highest gross revenue, whereas **Omar Khan** leads in target attainment percentage.
* **Business Value:** Provides clear benchmarks for sales coaching and helps leadership adjust regional targets or allocate stronger sales support to underperforming territories.

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used
* **Power BI Desktop:** Star-schema data modeling, custom dark-mode UI/UX layout, and interactive page navigation.
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Dynamic KPIs, variance calculations, CTR %, Gross Margin %, and Target Attainment %.
* **Data Visualization:** Custom tooltips, conditional formatting, geospatial mapping, and multi-page filtering.
